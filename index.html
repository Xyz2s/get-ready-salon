    Good to know

    Do I need an appointment?

    Booking is best, especially on weekends. Walk-ins are welcome when a chair is free.

    How do I pay?

    You pay at the studio after your visit. We take cards, cash and mobile wallets.

    Can I change or cancel?

    Yes. Call or email us at least 24 hours ahead and we’ll move or cancel your slot at no cost.

    Do you style for weddings and events?

    We do. Choose “Event and bridal” above, and mention the date and group size in your note.
